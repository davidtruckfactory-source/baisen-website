About Us

Shandong Baisen Machinery Co., Ltd.

Shandong Baisen Machinery is a professional commercial vehicle exporter based in Shandong, China. We specialize in providing reliable transportation solutions for customers worldwide, especially in Africa, the Middle East, Southeast Asia, and South America.

With years of experience in international trade and vehicle export, Baisen Machinery focuses on supplying high-quality new trucks, used trucks, semi trailers, buses, and truck spare parts to meet the diverse needs of global transportation businesses.
